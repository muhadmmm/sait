Vision + mission

Education, research and innovation.

Vision

World-class IT education and research.

Officially stated by the IT Division: to become a world leader in higher education and research in Information Technology.

Mission

Build competence for society, industry and entrepreneurship.

The IT Division describes its mission around state-of-the-art knowledge, competencies and virtues, innovative areas of study, industry and academic opportunities, and entrepreneurship.

Milestones, not a wall of text.

1978

School of Engineering begins its journey.

CUSAT's School of Engineering began in 1978 with part-time M.Tech programmes. The school later expanded into undergraduate engineering education and research.
